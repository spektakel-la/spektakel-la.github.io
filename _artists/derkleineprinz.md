  description: >
    Antoine de Saint-Exupéry created, with the Little Prince from Asteroid B 612, a sensitive ambassador of love and humanity. This stage adaptation invites festival visitors on a poetic and touching journey through the universe. Only through the taming of the fox does the Little Prince realize that humans can overcome their loneliness only with the help of friendship and love. "One sees clearly only with the heart. What is essential is invisible to the eye" is the central message of this modern fairy tale for children AND adults.

    Directed by Thomas Ecker; with Maya Finger / Vincent Kaiser, Ina Lehmann, Korbinian Greindl – Duration: approx. 45 minutes – Recommended for ages six and up
