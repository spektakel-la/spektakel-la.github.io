    The Picture of Dorian Gray by Oscar Wilde is one of the greatest classics of world literature. Its attractive title character remains forever young after a careless wish, while his portrait ages in his place and reveals each of his sins and moral failings as a terrible grimace.

    The Picture of Dorian Gray speaks directly to our time. The cult of eternal youth could hardly be portrayed more precisely, and faces smoothed with Botox and Instagram photos edited with Photoshop could hardly be mirrored more clearly than in Oscar Wilde's mysterious masterpiece.
